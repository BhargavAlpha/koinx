## Notes from Real Interviews

- **Meta (2025)**: AI-enabled coding round introduced - candidates use AI tooling during interview
- **E4/E5 (2025)**: Speed and accuracy on medium problems is the key differentiator
- Meta is **62% Medium-weighted** - highest among FAANG alongside Amazon
- Must solve a medium in **under 25 minutes** cleanly
- **Communication during coding** is heavily evaluated
- Follow-up questions on optimization are standard
- Binary Tree problems are disproportionately represented at Meta
- Recent trend: "Maze Solver" and graph traversal problems for AI coding rounds (2026)
