## Notes from Real Interviews

- **MTS (2024)**: "Snake and Ladder - minimum dice throws" (BFS problem) asked in onsite
- **LMTS (2025)**: Min Stack variation + drawing/explaining approach on excalidraw
- **Mid-Level (2025)**: Focus on Arrays, Strings, Hashmaps, Sliding Window, Two-Pointers
- Salesforce interviews are **medium-weighted** - mostly medium LeetCode-style problems
- Process: OA (HackerRank) -> Technical Coding -> System Design -> Behavioral
- Recommended prep: Neetcode 150, focusing on Arrays, Strings, Hashmaps, Sliding Window
- Explaining approach through drawing (excalidraw) makes a positive difference
