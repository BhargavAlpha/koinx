## Notes from Real Interviews

- **SDE-2 (2024)**: One medium-to-hard DP problem, solved in 40 minutes, then Java/Spring Boot questions
- **SDE-2 (2024)**: "Maximum sum of elements no two adjacent" - classic House Robber
- **SDE-2 (2025)**: Technical concepts in Java/Spring Boot + DSA coding
- **2026**: "Small Business Network: Degrees of Separation" - graph BFS problem
- Intuit interviews are **practical and product-minded**
- DP problems are asked more frequently at Intuit than other companies
- Java/Spring Boot technical knowledge is tested alongside DSA
- Process: 4-6 rounds - Recruiter -> OA -> Technical Coding -> Take-home/Build -> Design -> HM
- Quick feedback cycle - typically 2 weeks from HR communication to offer
- Focus on practical problem-solving over extreme algorithmic complexity
