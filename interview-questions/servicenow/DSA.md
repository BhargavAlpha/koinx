## Notes from Real Interviews

- **Associate SE (2024)**: Custom DSA problem - "array of zeros to ones with restricted positions"
- **SSE IC3 (2024)**: Theory on recursion + practical DSA coding
- **SSE (2024, Rejected)**: 4 rounds total over 30 days
- **Senior SE Frontend (2026)**: Flatten nested JSON object, matrix operations
- ServiceNow asks a mix of **standard LeetCode + custom/practical problems**
- Theory questions on recursion, complexity analysis are common
- **Sliding window** problems appear frequently
- Process: HR Screen -> DSA/Coding -> LLD/System Design -> Hiring Manager
- Interview experience feedback: Generally positive, quick with decisions
