## Notes from Real Interviews

- **Google L3 (2024-2025)**: Two coding rounds in phone screen, each 45 mins with 1-2 problems
- **Google Onsite**: 2 coding rounds + 1 system design + 1 behavioral (Googleyness)
- Google digs **deep into optimization** - always ask "Can you do better?"
- **Follow-up questions** are the norm - expect 2-3 follow-ups per problem
- Custom problems (not directly from LeetCode) are common - focus on patterns
- Graph problems are disproportionately represented compared to other FAANG
- Hard problems are more common at Google (~20% Hard vs 15% at others)
